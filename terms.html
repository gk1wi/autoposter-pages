1. Description of service

Autoposter is a content-publishing tool that helps creators schedule and automatically cross-post their short-form video content to TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube.

2. Authorized use

Autoposter publishes video content only to the account that has explicitly authorized the connection through the standard OAuth 2.0 flow of each platform (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube).

3. User responsibility

The account holder is solely responsible for the content published through Autoposter and must comply with the applicable Community Guidelines and Terms of Service of each connected platform, including TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

4. Revoking access

The account holder may disconnect Autoposter at any time from that platform's own account settings, which immediately stops any further publishing activity.
