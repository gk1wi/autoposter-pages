Autoposter

Automated short-form video publishing for content creators.

What it does

Autoposter schedules and automatically cross-posts short-form video clips to TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube on behalf of the creator who authorizes it. It replaces manually uploading each clip through every individual app, letting creators focus on making content instead of repeating the same upload steps across platforms.

How it works

  1. The creator connects their TikTok, Instagram, and/or YouTube account using each platform's official OAuth 2.0 login flow.
  2. The creator uploads their edited video clips to a private content queue.
  3. On a fixed schedule, Autoposter selects a clip and publishes it directly to the connected platform(s) using each platform's official publishing API.
  4. Once a clip is successfully published, it is removed from the queue automatically.

Features

  • Scheduled, automatic publishing
  • Official OAuth 2.0 authorization per platform
  • Per-platform enable/disable control
  • No manual re-uploading required
